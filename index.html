Community submissions

Share a photo from a Mandela landmark

Visitors can contribute photos from mapped Mandela landmarks around the world. Each submission is reviewed before anything appears publicly on the map.

How it works:
Choose a landmark → submit your photo → review by the team → approved photos may be added to the public map.
Approval-first workflow:
Photo submissions are handled through a Tally form and should be reviewed through eduoutreach.humanrights@gmail.com before publication.

To submit from a specific place, click a landmark on the map and choose “Submit a photo from this landmark.”
